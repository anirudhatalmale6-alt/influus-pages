Why it works

Being written about is not the same as advertising.

An advert interrupts. An article is looked up. The two are bought differently and they do not age the same way.

  • It is found by someone already searching for you
  • It keeps working long after the campaign budget stops
  • It is quotable — on your site, in a deck, in a pitch
  • It carries the outlet’s credibility, not yours

How a publication is chosen

Three questions, in this order.

Buying the highest number on the list is the most common mistake, and the most expensive one.

01

Who reads it, not who links to it

A publication with the right readership and a modest score beats a high-scoring site nobody in your market opens.

02

Does the link survive

A placement is worth what remains of it in a year. Do-follow, a permanent URL, and an outlet that keeps its archive.

03

Does the subject fit

An article about your field on a site about something else reads as paid, to a reader and to a search engine alike.

Domain Authority is a third-party score, not ours. 41 of the 159 listings state one; the rest do not, and a listing without a score is not a worse listing. That is why the catalogue filter on minimum DA only ever narrows to the ones that publish it.

Questions

Before you order.

What is DA, and why does it matter?

Domain Authority is a third-party score that estimates how much weight a domain carries in search. It is an indicator, not a guarantee. Where a listing states a DA we show it, and you can set a minimum DA when you filter the catalogue.

What does Do-Follow change?

A do-follow link passes ranking signal to your site; a nofollow link does not, but still carries the mention, the referral traffic and the credibility. 52 of the 159 listings are flagged Do-Follow, and you can filter on it.

Do I have to write anything?

No. Send the announcement and the angle and we write it. If you have a draft you would rather use, we edit it to the outlet’s format instead.

Can I order several placements at once?

Yes — that is what the catalogue page is for. Set a quantity on as many rows as you like and add the whole selection to the cart in one go.

Is the article permanent?

Placements are intended as permanent. An outlet remains free to edit or withdraw its own content, which is true of any earned coverage.

I need an outlet that is not listed.

Ask. The catalogue is what we hold ready; it is not the limit of what we can place.
