Questions

Before you order.

What is DA, and why does it matter?

Domain Authority is a third-party score that estimates how much weight a domain carries in search. It is an indicator, not a guarantee. Where a listing states a DA we show it, and you can set a minimum DA when you filter the catalogue.

What does Do-Follow change?

A do-follow link passes ranking signal to your site; a nofollow link does not, but still carries the mention, the referral traffic and the credibility. 52 of the 159 listings are flagged Do-Follow, and you can filter on it.

Do I have to write anything?

No. Send the announcement and the angle and we write it. If you have a draft you would rather use, we edit it to the outlet’s format instead.

Can I order several placements at once?

Yes — that is what the catalogue page is for. Set a quantity on as many rows as you like and add the whole selection to the cart in one go.

Is the article permanent?

Placements are intended as permanent. An outlet remains free to edit or withdraw its own content, which is true of any earned coverage.

I need an outlet that is not listed.

Ask. The catalogue is what we hold ready; it is not the limit of what we can place.

The whole catalogue is on one page, with filters and a single basket. Or tell us the goal and we build the selection for you.
